# Overview

Source: https://vibecodeforgoldwithgoogle.devpost.com/

Calling all U.S. developers! 📣 Become a Team USA Analyst with Gemini. Join Google Cloud for a first-of-its-kind Team USA hackathon to build the next generation of fan engagement tools!

Vibe code with Gemini to transform Team USA and publicly available data into interactive dashboards, web apps, or AI agents that showcase the collective power of Team USA.

Compete for up to $75,000 in prizes and the chance to be featured by Google Cloud!

#### Why join?

🧠 Learn & Experiment: Get hands-on with the latest in AI technology from Google.

🏆 Win Big: Compete for a share of $75,000 USD in cash prizes and Google Cloud credits!

🚀 Get Recognized: Feature your project in front of industry experts from Google and the developer community.

#### Get Started

1. Activate your [Google Cloud Account](https://developers.google.com/profile) (and get free credits to build!)
2. Get familiar with the [rules](https://vibecodeforgoldwithgoogle.devpost.com/rules "Rules"), [resources](https://vibecodeforgoldwithgoogle.devpost.com/resources), and [FAQs](https://vibecodeforgoldwithgoogle.devpost.com/details/faqs) for important rules around your project.
3. Explore the challenges and start brainstorming your project!
